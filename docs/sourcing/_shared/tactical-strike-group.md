The **Tactical Strike Group** contracts, taken from **InterSec Defense Solutions**. Contracts under
that same banner also yield the **Expired Quantanium Fuel Canister**, from Cargo A at the QV Extraction
stations — so it is worth clearing the containers rather than going for one item.

Contract wording, requirements and rewards are searchable at [SCMDB](https://scmdb.net/).
