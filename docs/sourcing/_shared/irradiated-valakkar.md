Irradiated Valakkar are fought at the **Storm Breaker Lazarus** locations. Which trophy drops depends
on the creature's stage — juvenile, adult or apex — so the target has to be picked before the fight,
not after.

- [Storm Breaker complete guide video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OOhGdMh_DyA).
