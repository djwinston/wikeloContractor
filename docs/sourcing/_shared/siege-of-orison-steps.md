1. Accept the **Siege of Orison** contract from Northrock Service Group.
2. Fight through to the mission's last location, where the final boss, **Mendo Ren**, has to be eliminated.
3. Loot Mendo Ren — he carries the full set: the BUL-H4 Helmet, BUL-H4 Armor, H4-PBF Ammo Carrier and Vendetta HMG.
4. On the way out of that location, search the lootboxes by the large cargo container.

The lootbox colour does not matter: the pieces turn up in blue and orange boxes alike.
