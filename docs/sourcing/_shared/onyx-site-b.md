Reward of **Jorrit Dossier: Project Hyperion** — the eighth contract of the **Onyx Facility** chain,
which unlocks once all seven earlier *Jorrit Dossier* contracts are done. It has you repeat the
Vanduul experiment in the **Site B** wing, and the sample it yields is an RCMBNT container.

Which of the nine variants you walk away with is decided by two choices during the run: the **beam**
you connect at the energy core sets the number, and the **experiment data** you collect sets the
series. The beams are labelled **1**, **2** and **3**, and the one you connect becomes the
experiment number.

The three data sets sit on different levels of the lab:

- **Gravity laboratory** gives the `PWL` series.
- **Medical office**, the one with the endless Sam respawn, gives the `RGL` series.
- **Biological processor** aboard the Blade ship gives the `XTL` series.
