The lootable containers sit at **Align & Mine** and **Storm Breaker** locations, with a much thinner
chance from the small boxes at **Derelict Outposts**. Box colour tells you which item pool it draws
from, so it is worth checking every container on site rather than a single type.

- [Storm Breaker One-Page Guide](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/community-hub/post/storm-breaker-one-page-guide-XTU7HaiJkZyFs) on Community Hub.
- [Storm Breaker complete guide video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OOhGdMh_DyA).
- [Align & Mine reference sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1EfbVwAoAfOgpVKwLBgvipvf8PfgnvX41uYLb2jkDIBc/) — per-location detail.
