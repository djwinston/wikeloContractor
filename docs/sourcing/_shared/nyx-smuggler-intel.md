One run feeds several unrelated-looking items: the Testudo Turfwar armour, Vanduul metal and plating,
the Boomtube Rocket Launcher and the Killshot Rifle all come out of it. Worth sweeping the whole
station rather than grabbing one thing and leaving.

Two names are in circulation for it — the community sheet files these drops under
**Vanduul-Tech Smugglers**, and the mission run at the QV logistic station in **Nyx** is called
**Retrieve Additional Smuggler Intel**. Community walkthroughs:

- [Vanduul-Tech Smugglers One-Page Guide (4.4)](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/community-hub/post/vanduul-tech-smugglers-one-page-guide-4-4-0-wegEjvpy1Bcl7) on Community Hub.
- [Vanduul-Tech Smugglers complete guide video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pcLc9CUt3WM).

Contract wording, requirements and rewards are searchable at [SCMDB](https://scmdb.net/).
