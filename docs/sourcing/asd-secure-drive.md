## Where to find it

Payout of the **Onyx Facility** investigation chain. The contracts are handed out in
**mobiGlas → Investigation** and unlock one after another. They all share the **Jorrit Dossier:**
prefix, so the whole chain sorts together in the list: it opens with *Onyx Personnel Files* and ends
with *Power Usage Data*, seven contracts in total.

## Step by step

1. Open **mobiGlas → Investigation** and accept *Jorrit Dossier: Onyx Personnel Files*.
2. Work through the chain — each completed contract unlocks the next one.
3. After the seventh contract (*Power Usage Data*), **15x ASD Secure Drive** land in your personal storage. This bulk payout happens **once**.
4. To keep farming, replay any single contract from the chain: each completion delivers **1x ASD Secure Drive**.

## Notes

- Finishing all seven also unlocks an **eighth** contract, *Jorrit Dossier: Project Hyperion* — the Site B investigation, and the source of the RCMBNT experiment samples.
