## Where to find it

Optional Yormandi encounter inside the **Site B** section of the **Onyx Facility**. One clear gives
**2x Yormandi Tongue**, alongside 4x Yormandi Eye.

## Step by step

1. Reach the Onyx Facility and head for the **Site B** section.
2. Enter the Yormandi area — see *Ways in* below for the two entrances.
3. Clear the encounter and loot **2x Yormandi Tongue**.

### Ways in

- **Shortcut through the lobby**, at the start of the facility — needs an **access key**, which is found while running Site B.
- **Entrance about halfway through Site B** itself — no access key required.

## Notes

- **Yormandi Eye** drops from the same clear, 4x per run.
